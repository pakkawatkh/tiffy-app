Step 2 • First-Launch Security
🛡️

2. Gatekeeper Notice

Because monkey-tools is free, open, and independently maintained without an expensive Apple Developer subscription ($99/yr), macOS Gatekeeper may show a warning ("cannot be opened because the developer cannot be verified"). Choose either quick option:

Option A: System Settings (Recommended)

Open the app once ➔ Go to System Settings > Privacy & Security ➔ Scroll down to Security ➔ Click "Open Anyway".

Option B: Terminal (1 Second)

Open Terminal and paste this one-liner command:

xattr -cr /Applications/monkey-tools.app

✓ 100% Safe: Unblocks only monkey-tools without compromising macOS security.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I open monkey-tools?

By default, press Cmd + Shift + V to toggle the clipboard history panel. You can also customize this hotkey in Settings at any time.

How does the highlight selection translator work?

Select/highlight any translatable text in any application (Safari, Chrome, Notes, Mail, Slack, Xcode, etc.). A small floating bubble (🌐) appears right beside your cursor. Click it to view the translation, listen to native speech, or replace original text.

Does monkey-tools store or upload my clipboard data?

No. monkey-tools is 100% private. All your clipboard data is stored strictly on your local disk in your user Application Support directory. No analytics, tracking, or network telemetry exist.

Which macOS versions are supported?

macOS 13.0 Ventura, macOS 14 Sonoma, and macOS 15 Sequoia (both Apple Silicon M-series and Intel Macs).

Why does macOS show a security warning when opening?

macOS Gatekeeper protects Macs by warning for apps downloaded from the internet that are self-signed. You can open it in seconds: go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security, and click "Open Anyway" (or run xattr -cr /Applications/monkey-tools.app in Terminal). This only allows monkey-tools without compromising system security.
