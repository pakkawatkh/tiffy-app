FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I open monkey-tools?

By default, press Cmd + Shift + V to toggle the clipboard history panel. You can also customize this hotkey in Settings at any time.

How does the highlight selection translator work?

Select/highlight any translatable text in any application (Safari, Chrome, Notes, Mail, Slack, Xcode, etc.). A small floating bubble (🌐) appears right beside your cursor. Click it to view the translation, listen to native speech, or replace original text.

Does monkey-tools store or upload my clipboard data?

No. monkey-tools is 100% private. All your clipboard data is stored strictly on your local disk in your user Application Support directory. No analytics, tracking, or network telemetry exist.

Which macOS versions are supported?

macOS 13.0 Ventura, macOS 14 Sonoma, and macOS 15 Sequoia (both Apple Silicon M-series and Intel Macs).
