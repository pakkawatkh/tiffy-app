Core Capabilities

Engineered for Precision & Speed

Everything you need to capture, trim, and share long web documents without opening external image editors.

📸

Auto-Scroll Canvas Stitching

Seamlessly sweeps through the entire page height, rendering each captured segment onto a high-DPI HTML5 canvas without seam lines or artifacts.

🚫

Sticky Header Protection

Intelligently discovers fixed and sticky headers on modern single-page apps (SPAs) and temporarily hides them so they don't repeat on intermediate slices.

✂️

8-Handle Studio Cropper

Fine-tune your screenshot in the dedicated editor tab. Drag 8 resize handles to frame any region with instant live resolution dimension tags.

📋

One-Click Clipboard Copy

Directly converts your cropped or full screenshot into an image/png blob on your OS clipboard. Press ⌘V to paste anywhere instantly.

Retina 2x High-DPI Support

100% Offline & Private

Quick Setup

Install in 30 Seconds

Capture Scroll is lightweight and runs unpacked directly in Chromium Developer Mode without waiting for Chrome Web Store approvals.

1

Download & Unzip

Download capture-scroll-v1.0.0.zip and double-click to extract the folder on your Mac.

↓ Download capture-scroll.zip
2

Open Extensions Tab

Navigate to the extensions manager in your favorite Chromium browser:

chrome://extensions
brave://extensions
edge://extensions
3

Enable Developer Mode

Toggle the Developer mode (โหมดนักพัฒนา) switch located in the top-right corner of the page.

4

Load Unpacked & Pin

Click Load unpacked (โหลดส่วนขยายที่คลายการบีบอัดแล้ว) and select the unzipped capture-scroll directory. Pin the icon to your toolbar!

Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Which browsers are supported?

Capture Scroll works natively on all modern Chromium-based browsers including Google Chrome, Brave Browser, Microsoft Edge, Arc, Vivaldi, and Opera.

Can I customize the keyboard shortcut?

Yes. The default shortcut is Command + Shift + S (or Ctrl + Shift + S on Windows). You can change this at any time in your browser's shortcut settings (chrome://extensions/shortcuts).

Does Capture Scroll store my screenshots in the cloud?

Never. Screenshots are created on an in-memory HTML5 Canvas and cached temporarily in local IndexedDB only while the Preview Studio tab is open. Once you close the tab, all image buffers are automatically purged.

Is Capture Scroll completely free?

Yes, Capture Scroll is 100% free and open-source, created by Pakkawat.k as part of the Tiffy productivity utility suite.
